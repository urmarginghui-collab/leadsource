How the numbers work
  • Which leads: contacts created in Respond.io on the chosen date (00:00 to 23:59, Malaysia time).
  • Lifecycle: each lead's lifecycle stage right now (at the last sync time shown at the top). Each lead is counted once. Appointment = APPT stage.
  • New Leads / Total Leads: every contact created that day from that region, dataset or ad, whatever its stage. Rates are Not Interested ÷ New Leads, Appointment ÷ New Leads, Show Up ÷ New Leads.
  • Dataset: the contact field FB Sales Campaign. Ad: the contact field Ads ID. Respond.io stores only the Ad ID, not the ad name.
  • Region: read from the dataset name (the "$" in front is ignored): contains Penang → PENANG, Selangor → SELANGOR, Johor or JB → JB. Anything else, including leads with no FB Sales Campaign, is UNCLASSIFIED. Nothing is guessed.
  • Low-quality ad: at least 3 new leads and the highest Not Interested rate. Quality ad: at least 3 new leads and the highest (Appointment + Show Up) rate.
  • Customer names and phone numbers are not stored here. Click Open #ID to see the lead in Respond.io (needs a Respond.io login).
  • Data updates every night at 11:50pm and when Ginghui refreshes. This page reloads the data every 5 minutes.
